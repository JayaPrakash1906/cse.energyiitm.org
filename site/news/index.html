The Centre invited applications for a Project Technician to run and maintain its shared high-temperature, high-pressure (HT-HP) reactor facility, with operation up to supercritical conditions, supporting several research groups. Event / Coal India Limited headquarters, Kolkata

CIL

Knowledge-sharing session with Coal India Limited in Kolkata

Coal India Limited hosted a day-long knowledge-sharing and interactive session with the Centre at its headquarters in Kolkata, attended by CIL leadership, IIT Madras faculty, officers from CIL subsidiaries and the Centre's technical collaborators. Shri Achyut Ghatak, Director (Technical), spoke on the value of collaboration between industry and academia. IIT Madras faculty presented work on the circular economy, repurposing coal mines, coal to advanced materials, and gasification for energy security.

23–25Jul 2026

Event / IIT Madras

SustainX 2026: Circularity in Energy, Earth and Environment

The three-day annual conference of the School of Sustainability, IIT Madras, organised with the Centre for Circularity (CRRET), brought together researchers, students, industry practitioners and policymakers around six themes, with more than 25 resource persons and 100 abstracts. The conference was supported by the Centre, and six members of our research team presented work on coal-derived carbon materials, coal mine wastewater treatment, battery recycling and polymer composites that use coal as an additive.

Presenting my poster at SustainX was such a rewarding experience. The best part was receiving real-time feedback and fresh perspectives from academic and industry experts who are truly committed to solving sustainability challenges together.

Shubham Jaiswal, Postdoctoral Researcher

Event / IIT Madras

3rd International Conference on Energy Conversion and Storage (IECS 2026)

The Centre sponsored the third International Conference on Energy Conversion and Storage at IIT Madras, co-organised with the University of St Andrews, United Kingdom, and Ming Chi University of Technology, Taiwan. Prof. Kothandaraman Ramanujam was the convenor, with Prof. Satyanarayanan Seshadri, Prof. Venkataraman Thangadurai, Prof. Jose Rajan and Prof. Chun-Chen Yang as co-convenors. Sixty-two speakers covered energy storage, sustainable materials and battery recycling across plenary lectures, keynote addresses, invited talks and technical sessions.
