We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA and the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW).

What we have done

  • Text and interface colours meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1.
  • Research areas are shown with a pattern and a word as well as a colour.
  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, and focus is always visible.
  • Diagrams and maps have text equivalents on the same page.
  • Pages reflow to a width of 320 pixels and to 400% zoom. Wide tables scroll sideways inside their own frame; the news milestones turn into a vertical list.
  • Drawings build once as they come into view and then stay still. This is switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • The site works without JavaScript.

Known limitations

  • Some portrait photographs are low resolution. We are replacing them.
  • The site is currently in English only.

Feedback

If you find a barrier on this website, write to us at contact.cse@energy.iitm.ac.in. We aim to respond within five working days.
