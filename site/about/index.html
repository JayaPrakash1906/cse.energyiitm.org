About the Centre

The CIL Centre for Sustainable Energy is a Centre of Excellence at IIT Madras in partnership with Coal India Limited, the world's single largest coal producing company. The Centre is reimagining India's coal sector for a future powered by clean energy, advanced materials and sustainable infrastructure.

Aligned with India's 2070 net-zero vision, the Centre transforms coal-sector strengths into research with national impact.

01

How the Centre began

Signed on 22 October 2025, adapted from the Press Information Bureau release.

On 22 October 2025, IIT Madras and Coal India Limited signed a Memorandum of Understanding to establish the Centre for Sustainable Energy. Prof. V. Kamakoti, Director, IIT Madras, and Mr. Achyut Ghatak, Director (Technical), Coal India Limited, signed the agreement in the presence of Mr. P. M. Prasad, Chairman, Coal India Limited, and the leadership of both organisations.

The Centre was set up as a Centre of Excellence for research, innovation and technology development in sustainable energy. Its purpose is to strengthen India's long-term energy security while supporting the national commitment to net-zero emissions by 2070, by bringing together institutions and industries from India and abroad to find economically viable and environmentally responsible ways to use the country's energy resources.

Coal meets more than 85 per cent of India's energy needs, and it also holds a key to the country's transition. As India's foremost energy resource provider, Coal India is placed to become an energy provider of the future through its assets: land for renewable energy, spent mines for repurposing and storage, and coal as a feedstock for clean fuels, chemicals and high-value materials.

The Centre is led by Prof. Satyanarayanan Seshadri, Head of the Energy Consortium, IIT Madras, and Prof. Rajnish Kumar, Head of the School of Sustainability, IIT Madras. By joining Coal India's industrial scale and operating experience with the research of IIT Madras, it aims for solutions that are technically feasible, economically viable, environmentally sustainable and socially responsible.

Its first projects span coal-derived materials, clean processes and new uses for mines. See the research

The strategic opportunity

Coal India's key assets, and what research can make of them.

  1. 01Re-energise the land

    Land Assets

    Vast land resources across India can support large-scale renewable energy projects, driving clean power generation at industrial scale.

    • Research area: Mines
  2. 02Transform the resource

    Coal Resources

    Leveraging coal's carbon chemistry can unlock the development of advanced materials, clean fuels and functional composites, contributing to sustainable innovation.

    • Research area: Materials
    • Research area: Processes
  3. 03Repurpose the mine

    Spent Mines

    Decommissioned mines, with their geological stability and existing infrastructure, present untapped opportunities for energy storage, sustainable computing and circular repurposing.

    • Research area: Mines

Mission, model and impact

Research anchored in Coal India's challenges, carried from foundational science to deployment, and fed back by what happens in the field.

01Mission
Industry-led researchAll research is anchored in challenges of direct strategic relevance to CIL, supported by a dynamic feedback loop that ensures continuous alignment with evolving industrial needs.
02Model
Hub-and-spoke collaborationIIT Madras serves as the central hub, coordinating with CIL, peer institutions and industry partners across a strategically managed portfolio of projects.

Technology to deploymentProjects span the full innovation spectrum, from foundational science to pilot-scale demonstration, so that discoveries translate into real-world solutions.
