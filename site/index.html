Welcome to the Centre for Sustainable Energy

IIT Madras, Chennai12.99° N, 80.23° E

A Centre of Excellence at IIT Madras in partnership with Coal India Limited, turning the strengths of India's coal sector into research for the country's 2070 net-zero goal.

CIL's Future: Three Approaches

Illustrative images.

  1. 01Land Assets

    Re-energise the land

    Vast land resources across India can support large-scale renewable energy projects, driving clean power generation at industrial scale.

    • Research area: Mines
  2. 02Coal Resources

    Transform the resource

    Leveraging coal's carbon chemistry can unlock the development of advanced materials, clean fuels and functional composites, contributing to sustainable innovation.

    • Research area: Materials
    • Research area: Processes
  3. 03Spent Mines

    Repurpose the mine

    Decommissioned mines, with their geological stability and existing infrastructure, present untapped opportunities for energy storage, sustainable computing and circular repurposing.

    • Research area: Mines

02

How the Centre works

Research anchored in Coal India's challenges, carried from foundational science to deployment, and fed back by what happens in the field.

01Mission
Industry-led researchAll research is anchored in challenges of direct strategic relevance to CIL, supported by a dynamic feedback loop that ensures continuous alignment with evolving industrial needs.
02Model
Hub-and-spoke collaborationIIT Madras serves as the central hub, coordinating with CIL, peer institutions and industry partners across a strategically managed portfolio of projects.
03Impact
Technology to deploymentProjects span the full innovation spectrum, from foundational science to pilot-scale demonstration, so that discoveries translate into real-world solutions.
